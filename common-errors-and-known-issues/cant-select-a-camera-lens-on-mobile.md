# Can't select a camera lens on mobile

1. Open the [VDO.Ninja native app](../steves-helper-apps/native-mobile-app.md).
2. Choose **BACK CAMERA**, **FRONT CAMERA**, or an extra lens such as **BACK ULTRA-WIDE** when it appears.
3. Check the preview before starting the stream. The available lenses depend on the phone and its operating system.

For zoom, focus, exposure, and white balance, enable **Professional Camera Controls** in the app's advanced settings. A zoom slider may crop the picture rather than switch physical lenses.

### Digital zoom

In the browser, [Digital zoom](../source-settings/effects.md) crops the picture. It does not unlock another camera lens.

### Smartphones that support more than one rear camera

In the browser, open the **Video Source** list and compare its choices with the native app. Different browsers and OS versions may expose different cameras, even on the same phone.

### Publish through a server

The VDO.Ninja app includes WHIP publishing. Use **Additional outputs > Meshcast** for built-in distribution, or **Custom WHIP** for a service's publishing URL. See [WHIP setup](../steves-helper-apps/native-mobile-app.md#whip-publishing). Choosing WHIP changes delivery, not which lenses the phone exposes.

### Lens adapter

A clip-on lens can change the field of view without changing the selected camera. Check that it lines up with your phone's lens and does not block other cameras.

### USB external cameras

The Android app supports compatible UVC webcams and HDMI capture adapters through **USB CAMERA**. Connect the device, grant USB permission, and use a powered hub if needed. See [USB camera setup](../steves-helper-apps/native-mobile-app.md#usb-camera).

The VDO.Ninja iOS app does not expose USB camera input. Its USB audio support is separate.
