Record a mixed scene

  1. Open Mixer, create a room, and invite two camera guests.
  2. Select a layout that shows both guests and confirm their audio is enabled in the scene.
  3. In Mixer Settings → Recording, choose whether to Show guest names in recordings. This saved setting applies to both recording options; names are off by default. Open a new recording window after changing it.
  4. Choose Record Scene, or More Outputs → Record to Disk → Open Output Window.
  5. Click Start Recording in the scene window. Select that browser tab and enable Share tab audio.
  6. Recording captures the complete scene, including both videos. The recording controls disappear. Use the browser's Stop sharing control to finish and save.
  7. Play the saved file: both guests should be visible and audible, without the publishing panel or recording controls. Start another recording to confirm the controls work again.

If no audio was shared, cancel the warning and try again with tab audio enabled, or explicitly choose to record without sound.
