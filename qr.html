Start a connection

Connect two devices without the WebSocket handshake server. Start on one device, scan or paste its code on the other, then send the reply code back to the first device. Keep both pages open until connected.

On a phone, the other person can just point their camera app at the QR code you get next - it opens this page for them with the offer already loaded.

Once connected, either person can start their camera and microphone separately. Scanning a code does not share your camera. Across different networks, a STUN or TURN server may still be needed.
