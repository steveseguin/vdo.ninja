## Recent highlights (rolling)

* **iOS — September 9, 2026, version 5.0.101**
  Added RTMP/SRT streaming, advanced external encoder controls, and a minimum-bitrate hint for VDO.Ninja camera streams. Screen-share quality labels and restart guidance were also improved. See the [App Store version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vdo-ninja/id1607609685).

* **iOS — August and September 2026**
  Recent releases added screen-share camera overlay and professional controls, and improved WHIP/WHEP delivery, ReplayKit app audio, and screen-sharing reliability. Availability depends on the app version, device, and capture mode.

* **Android — September 16, 2026 store update**
  Restored app-specific screen sharing on supported devices and improved camera startup/switching, signaling, and screen/audio track handling. See the [Google Play release notes](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=flutter.vdo.ninja).

* **Updated setup and quality guidance**
  The [native app guide](../steves-helper-apps/native-mobile-app.md) now covers platform-specific screen audio, camera overlay, publishing destinations, and current controls. The [quality guide](../guides/improving-quality-of-the-native-app.md) explains iOS screen-share modes, Android camera presets, frame-rate limits, and where viewer URL parameters apply.
